# PF2e - Character Sheet

> Overview: [Pathfinder 2e](/help/pf2e).

`sheet` - View your full PF2e sheet.
`sheet <name>` - View another character's sheet (requires `view_sheet` permission, or viewing yourself).
`sheet/combat` - Short combat sheet (HP, AC, weapon, saves, Perception, conditions).
`csheet` - Shortcut for `sheet/combat`.
`sheet/combat <name>` - Combat sheet for another character (same permission rules).

## What the full sheet shows

* Identity (ancestry, heritage, class, level)
* Abilities and modifiers
* AC (from equipped armor when present)
* HP, speed, hero points, focus points
* Saves and Perception
* Skills
* Feats and conditions

## What the combat sheet shows

* HP and temp HP
* AC from equipped armor
* Primary equipped weapon and attack modifier
* Saves and Perception
* Conditions

Equip armor and weapons with [Gear](/help/gear) so AC and attack lines stay current.
